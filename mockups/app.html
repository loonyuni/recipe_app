Pasta With Green Pea Sauce

Fusilli tossed with caramelized leeks and a bright blender sauce of green peas, mint, garlic and lemon, finished with pecorino.

PastaVegetarianSpring
Made 2× · last 3 wk ago

Pea Sauce

  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups frozen green peas

Method

  1. Cook the oil and garlic over medium-low until it just begins to caramelize.
  2. Boil the peas 2 minutes, then blend with the garlic oil, mint, water, lemon, and salt until smooth.
